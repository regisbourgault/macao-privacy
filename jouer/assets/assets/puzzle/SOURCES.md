# Built-in puzzle pictures — sources and licences

All twelve images are photographs of two-dimensional paintings or prints whose artists died
more than a century ago, so the works are in the public domain worldwide; faithful photographs
of such works are themselves in the public domain (PD-Art). Each file was downloaded from
Wikimedia Commons on 2026-10-23 after checking the licence stated on its Commons page, then
resized to 1600 px on the long side and re-encoded as JPEG (quality 70, 50 for
`monet_nympheas.jpg`). `seurat_grande_jatte.jpg` was also trimmed by about 2 % on each side to
remove the painted border.
